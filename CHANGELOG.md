## [0.1.0a4] - 2026-10-16

### Added

- Declared detectors: a policy document can carry a `detectors:` section with `pattern` detectors (RE2 patterns with an optional checksum, one of `luhn`, `inn`, `snils`, `ogrn` and `iban`, context and exclude words, and JSON-key globs), `terms` detectors (lists of up to 5000 terms of 200 characters, matched as whole words whatever the case and the Latin/Cyrillic look-alike letters, with `*` standing for any ending, without any extra and in time linear in the text) and `remote` detectors (a service that speaks the analyze contract, with its entity types mapped to a class and subtype). A document is checked when it is loaded: patterns have to compile, the `match` and `no_match` examples of a declaration have to hold, an endpoint carries no credentials, and a document has at most 20 detectors, 3 of them remote. `DeclaredDetectors` builds the detectors per tenant and keeps them per tenant and declaration; a declared detector is named `declared:<id>`, and its cached results are scoped to the tenant and to the digest of its declaration. See `docs/detectors.md`.
- Per-call detector selection (`donkit_guard.selection`): a detector runs only when a rule that matched the context could use its findings, judged from its classes and subtypes against `finding_class` and `finding_subtype`. It runs synchronously when a rule with an action other than `audit` could use it, and in the background when only `audit` rules could.
- Background checks: the detectors that only `audit` rules can use scan while the decision goes back, and what they find is recorded afterwards as a `FindingsEvent` under the decision's `decision_id`, after the decision event and only when that event was written. Besides the locations, classes and `audit` rule ids of the findings and the background detectors that failed, it carries `display_args_redacted`, the readable argument previews of a tool call that its decision held back while the check ran (see Changed). `Decision.unchecked_detectors` lists the detectors whose findings a decision did not get. A decision that asks for approval of a tool call waits for its background detectors and, when one failed, hides every argument value. `EngineSettings.max_background_checks` (200) bounds the checks in flight, `Engine.drain()` waits for them and `Engine.aclose()` gives them `EngineSettings.background_drain_s` (5 seconds). The findings of a check that has not finished when the process ends, or when its event loop closes, are lost: a host that runs a new event loop per call evaluates with `defer=False`.
- A background lane in `DetectorRunner`: `run(..., background=True)` runs local detectors on `background_workers` workers of their own (default 1; with `ExecutorKind.PROCESS` that is one more worker process) and keeps at most `max_background_remote` remote calls in flight per event loop (default 16). A background scan's deadline starts once it has its turn, and a local one gets at least `Budget.background_min_ms` (1000 ms), so background work never delays a synchronous scan or makes it time out (with `ExecutorKind.SYNC`, which has no workers, local detectors run on the event loop and a background scan still holds it up). Background checks and the evaluations that observe mode defers take this lane, and a background check starts before the synchronous scan, so an approval card waits for the longer of the two scans, not for both in turn. The lane has no priority within itself: the background scans of an approval card wait behind other background work.
- The analyze contract is public: `docs/remote-detectors.md`, the OpenAPI file `docs/contract/analyze-v1.openapi.json` (generated from the models by `scripts/gen_contract.py`, which CI runs with `--check`), a conformance check (`python -m donkit_guard.conformance <url>` and `check_service`) and an example service in `examples/remote-detector`.
- `donkit_guard.http.HttpAnalyzeClient`, a hardened client for the analyze contract: no proxy or certificate settings from the environment, no redirects, `verify` for a private CA, an uncompressed answer only (it sends `Accept-Encoding: identity` and refuses a compressed answer with `unsupported content encoding`), a cap on the size of the answer counted in bytes as they are received (1 MiB by default), a service's own error cut to 200 characters, and every failure returned as a failed response instead of raised. `HttpConnections` keeps one connection pool per origin for the clients that share it through `HttpAnalyzeClient(..., connections=...)`, and `tls_verify(ca_bundle)` turns a CA file or directory into its certificate setting. The analyze clients of a `DeclaredDetectors` share one `HttpConnections`, made with the first remote detector and closed by `DeclaredDetectors.aclose()`, and a missing or unreadable `ca_bundle` raises `OSError` when `DeclaredDetectors` is constructed. Connections are bound to the event loop that made them, so a host that evaluates on several event loops needs one `DeclaredDetectors` per loop.
- Host hooks for remote detectors: `CredentialResolver.token(tenant_id, name, endpoint)` turns the credential name of a declaration into a token for the endpoint it would be sent to, so that a host can bind a credential to its tenant and its service, and `EndpointGuard` (default `AllowAllEndpoints`) refuses an address by raising `EndpointRejected`. Credentials are opt-in: without a resolver none resolves, and a remote detector whose declaration names one fails with `credential not found` and sends nothing. `EnvCredentialResolver` reads `DONKIT_GUARD_CREDENTIAL_<NAME>` and suits only a host that writes every policy document itself, because any document may name any of these variables and have it sent to the endpoint it declares. The hooks are exported from the package root together with `DeclaredDetectors`, and `build_quickstart_guard` takes a `declared` argument.
- Optional extras `re2` (`google-re2`, for pattern detectors) and `http` (`httpx`, for remote detectors and the HTTP client). The base dependencies are unchanged, and importing `donkit_guard` needs neither.
- Optional detector attributes, read through accessors in `donkit_guard.detectors.base`: `subtypes`, `cache_scope`, `timeout_ms`, `cache_ttl_s` and `required` (`False` makes a failure count as an unchecked detector instead of a failed check). `DetectorResultCache.put` takes a lifetime per entry.
- `Segment.key()`, the JSON key a leaf sits under, and `redact_display_args(..., redact_all=True)`, which blacks out every value.
- Documentation: `docs/detectors.md`, `docs/remote-detectors.md`, the declared detectors in `docs/threat-model.md` and the example policy `examples/policies/tenant-detectors.yaml`.

### Changed

- `AnalyzeSegment.lang` is `str | None` (it was `"ru"` or `"en"`, defaulting to `"en"`): any language code is allowed, and the `guard-pii` service analyzes a code it does not know, or none, as English.
- `AnalyzeResponse` and `AnalyzeSpan` ignore fields they do not know, so a service can extend its answer without breaking its callers.
- `Decision.detector_versions` lists the detectors selected for the call instead of every configured detector, and a detector that no matching rule can use is no longer run. `DetectorRunner.run` and `DetectorRunner.versions` accept a set of detectors, `DetectorRunner.detectors` is public, and a remote detector that declares `timeout_ms` is given that deadline.
- The decision a host receives no longer reflects what the detectors that only `audit` rules can use find, because they run after it: under the default policy `explanation.rule_ids` lose `pii-audit` and `injection-audit`, `explanation.finding_classes` and `explanation.locations` lose `pii` and `injection` (personal data in a model request to a non-platform destination is still found before the decision, because a `mask` rule uses it), and `critical` drops for such decisions, so in enforce mode a failed write of their decision event no longer turns them into an `audit_unavailable` deny. Those findings come in the decision's `FindingsEvent`; `defer=False` gives a decision that includes them.
- The argument previews of a tool call (`Decision.display_args_redacted`, which the approval card and the `DecisionEvent` carry) show every value blacked out whole while a detector has not reported on them: while a background check is still running, and when a detector failed or was not started. When the running check was the only reason, the readable previews follow in `FindingsEvent.display_args_redacted`, also when the background detectors found nothing, and a host's audit view shows those in place of the decision's; a failed background detector leaves the values hidden.
- A failure of a detector that only `audit` rules can use no longer fails the decision: in enforce mode it was a deny with the code `detector_unavailable`, and it is now listed as unchecked, in `Decision.unchecked_detectors` when the decision waited for the detector and in the `FindingsEvent` when it ran in the background.
- In observe mode `Engine.evaluate` answers at once with an `allow` whose explanation code is `observe_deferred`, and records the full decision afterwards under the same `decision_id`. Pass `defer=False` to evaluate before returning, as before. An `ExecutionEvent` recorded for such an answer before its decision is written follows the `DecisionEvent`: the engine chains the write, logs a sink error in it instead of raising, and `Engine.drain()` and `Engine.aclose()` cover it.
- `AuditEvent` also includes `FindingsEvent`: an `AuditSink` that handles events by type has to accept `findings`.

### Fixed

- `Decision.masked_locations` is cleared together with `masked_segments` when an approval outcome turns a decision into a deny and when a failed audit write does (`audit_unavailable`), so it is empty whenever `masked_segments` is.

## [0.1.0a3] - 2026-09-25

### Added

- `Decision.masked_locations`: the spans behind `masked_segments` (segment, JSON pointer, offsets, class, subtype), so a host can show the person who wrote a text which parts of it the destination did not receive. Empty whenever `masked_segments` is — in observe mode and on a deny.

## [0.1.0a2] - 2026-09-19

### Added

- `EngineSettings.preview_chars`: how many characters of each argument an approval card and an audit row keep (default 256). A host whose approvers read the card before allowing a write raises it so the content being written is visible in full.

## [0.1.0a1] - 2026-09-18

### Added

- Core package: attribute-based `GuardContext`, segment `Payload` model whose segment id hashes the source, the JSON pointer and the text (so two leaves holding the same value stay addressable apart), versioned YAML policies with layers and a shipped default policy, two-phase `Engine` combining verdicts on the strictness lattice `deny > require_approval > mask > allow`, deterministic masking that keeps JSON valid, asynchronous one-time approvals (`ApprovalFlow`), audit event models, adapter protocols and in-memory implementations, `Guard` facade and `build_quickstart_guard()`.
- Detectors: `secrets@1` (gitleaks rule set as data plus detect-secrets-derived patterns, keyword-anchored scanning loop verified against the gitleaks binary, low-confidence entropy candidates as audit-only spans), `pii-checksum@1` (SNILS, INN, OGRN, credit cards, phones, e-mail, contextual passport numbers; a context-free numeric identifier scores 0.4 and is audited, a formatted one or one with a context word scores 1.0), `pii-ner@1` (remote, over the `guard-pii` service), `injection-heuristic@1` (RU/EN, detection signal only); `DetectorRunner` with size-dependent budgets and a content-addressed result cache.
- Provenance: `Findings` and `Decision.detector_versions` carry `name@major` together with the hash of the pattern set that produced the spans, and that hash is part of the cache key, so an edited rule set cannot serve superseded results.
- Approval and audit previews: the engine redacts the tool arguments once, with every detected span blacked out and a bounded number of entries, and the approval card, the decision and the audit event all carry that same preview.
- Approval resolution: a rejected queued approval answers the host's next retry with `approval_rejected`, an approval invalidated by a policy change is marked stale in the store, and the outcome the host acts on is audited under the `decision_id` of the decision that asked for it.
- Policy composition and validation: composing documents takes the strictest `mode` and `unsupported_content` and the smallest `approval_ttl_s` and `segment_limit_bytes`; rule ids are unique across the whole bundle; a `mask` rule must select findings; `effect: unknown` is rejected in a selector because unknown effects are gated as `write`.
- Fail-closed behaviour in enforce mode: a policy error is a deny with code `policy_error`, a detector timeout or error `detector_unavailable`, a failed write of a critical audit event `audit_unavailable`, and every deny clears the masked segments.
- `guard-pii` service (`services/pii`, `docker/Dockerfile.pii`): Presidio + spaCy ru/en models + tuned Russian recognizers, per-entity thresholds, a bounded analysis pool, an analyzer failure reported to the core as a detector failure, a non-root image, offline by construction.
- Documentation: architecture, policy reference, example strict policy, third-party notices.
